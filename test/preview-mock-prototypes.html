Phase 0 · pick one per family

Sixteen mocks for a seven-column rail

The preview rail grew from 300px to ~700px, but its two mocks were designed for the old squeeze. Eight redesigns of the Appearance chrome mock, eight of the Measuring vignette — every one rendered inside the rail width it will actually live in.

Every frame is the real rail. 700px raised panel, real theme tokens, chrome at its true 11px scale. Click the swatch dots to re-theme any prototype.

Two picks. One A and one M — they're independent, and hybrids across numbers are legal as always.

Pick by the tradeoff line, not the prettiest render. Fidelity, maintenance, and what each mock teaches all differ.
