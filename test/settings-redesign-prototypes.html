Twelve ways to redesign Settings around Measuring

Same content in every one — the theme picker, six measuring settings (unit, precision, pill placement, guides, quiet overlay, zero pills), and a preview. What differs is who gets the space, and how the preview answers the setting you're touching.

These are live. Every control works: flip a measuring setting and the redline vignette updates; click a theme and the prototype re-themes. Defaults everywhere are the extension's current behaviour.

The vignette is honest geometry. Each line spans the gap it claims to measure; true gaps are 46.4px and 32.6px, so precision and unit visibly change the readout.

Pick by the tradeoff line, not the prettiest render — every one of these can be made pretty. Tell me a number (or a hybrid) and I'll build it.

01

Rows and a rail that answers you

The straight-line evolution of the current page. Settings as rows in the scroll column; the sticky rail swaps its content to whatever section you're in — chrome mock for Appearance, redline vignette for Measuring.

Wins
Zero relearning — it's today's page with the rows the layout always promised. The swap gives the rail a reason to be sticky: the preview is never about the wrong thing.
Costs
The swap is a mode, and modes can surprise: change a theme, drift down, and the chrome mock you wanted to watch is gone. The 300px rail still crops the toolbar.

07

The diagram is the control

No settings column for measuring at all — you configure the vignette by touching it. Click the pill to move it, the guide to toggle guides, the selection to quiet the overlay, the readout chips for unit and precision.

Wins
Direct manipulation: the thing you change is the thing that changes, so the preview problem dissolves — there is nothing but preview. Shortest possible distance between intent and effect.
Costs
Invisible affordances need the hint line; toggling something off leaves a ghost target you have to know to re-click. Accessibility is a from-scratch build — the diagram needs a parallel keyboard surface.

Hybrids are legal — "01's rows with 10's proportions", "04's stage over 05's sheet". The measuring defaults in every prototype are the extension's shipping behaviour: px, whole, beside, guides on, quiet off, zero pills on.
